# toroidal_3loop_p75_RH_shaft

3-loop toroidal prop, 110 mm, pitch 75 mm, RH.

| Check | Value |
|---|---|
| Printed volume / mass | 13.1 cm^3, ~16 g at 1.2 g/cm^3 |
| Gap between the legs of one loop | 8.5 mm |
| Gap between neighbouring loops | 2.9 mm |
| Hub | 20 OD x 43 long, D-bore for a 5 mm shaft (4.5 across the flat), M5 nylock on the shaft end |
| Rpm at 1.5 m/s (30 % slip) | 1663 |
| Rpm at 3.0 m/s (40 % slip) | 3880 |
| Drive | 1:1 belt from a ~380 KV outrunner: 6840 rpm no-load at 18 V |

Gaps under ~2 mm print poorly and trap weed; open them with loop_dz / loop_dtheta / skew.
Balance every print on a knife-edge or magnetic balancer before running it.
