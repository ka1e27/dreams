# The V3 carrier, rev D, and the pod board

We finished rev D of the V3 carrier board and updated the sensor-pod board after a design review.

![The V3 carrier board, rev D](img/upd-2026-10-02-carrier-rev-d.png)
![The sensor-pod board](img/v3-pod-iso-900.png)

## The carrier board, rev D

The carrier is V3's main board. It hosts the Raspberry Pi 5 and the Teensy 4.1, talks to both motor controllers over CAN, and splits power into three isolated domains.

Rev D was re-placed and re-routed so the Pi 5 sits fully on the board: 130.6 × 101.5 mm, 4 layers and 126 parts. A design review added protection, status LEDs and test points, fixed the polarity of the XT60 battery connector, and removed 11 parts it didn't need. Design-rule and schematic checks all pass.

## The sensor-pod board, rev B

The sensor pod is tethered to the boat and samples the water at the surface: pH, dissolved oxygen, TDS, turbidity and temperature. Its board is 48 × 63 mm with 2 layers, and it talks to the carrier over RS-485. Rev B came out of a design review and was re-placed on a neat grid.
