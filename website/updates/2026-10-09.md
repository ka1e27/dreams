# A website, a logo and two new teammates

We launched this website, Nolan drew our logo, and two new members joined the team. We also sized V3's drive and started looking for sponsors.

## The website

The site went live with the boat, a build log of every version, the V3 board in 3D, and a simulated V2 command center you can try in your browser.

## The logo

Nolan drew the new logo by hand: the word is the hull, and the D is the bow.

## New teammates

Nolan Ting and Caleb Kong joined the team. Both study Mechanical Engineering at Northeastern, class of 2029.

## V3's drive

A 3 ft catamaran needs about 200–300 W per side at survey speed, so we're planning belt-driven 5065-size motors mounted on deck.

## Sponsors

We started reaching out to sponsors for parts, PCB fabrication and machining.
