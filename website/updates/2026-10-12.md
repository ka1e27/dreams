# V3.1, a starter board to get on the water

The full V3 carrier board is big and built around a Teensy 4.1. V3.1 is a smaller interim board that gets the boat running sooner, and it's our first try of the Pico-based electronics planned for the full V3.

![V3.1 in 3D, from the top](img/upd-2026-10-12-iso-1600.jpg)

## What's on it

- A Raspberry Pi 5 for navigation and mission logic, and a Raspberry Pi Pico 2 for real-time control. They talk over USB.
- One 10S Li-ion pack (30–42 V) powers everything, through a fuse and reverse-polarity protection. The Pico watches the pack voltage.
- CAN for the two drive motor controllers, plus PWM outputs for the drive motors and the winch.
- An RC receiver input for manual control, and a GPS with a built-in compass.
- An SHT41 sensor for the air inside the hull, a Qwiic port for a TMP119 temperature sensor, and two hull leak probes.
- The tether to the sensor pod, carrying RS-485 data and 15 V power, and a header for a second Pico.

## Layout

The board is 98 × 67 mm, just bigger than the Pi, which mounts face down on standoffs above it. To keep that space clear, every tall part went underneath: the Pico on 8.5 mm sockets, both power modules and every capacitor. Nothing under the Pi is taller than about 9 mm, and the cable connectors sit along the edges, outside the Pi.
