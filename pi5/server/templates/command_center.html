⚙ MOTOR CONFIGURATION

PER-MOTOR TRIM
Two different-Kv motors give unequal thrust at equal command, so the boat crabs. Lower the faster motor's gain until it tracks straight (1.00 = no trim). The heading loop nulls the rest.
ESC CONFIGURATION (AM32)
⚠ Experimental / hardware-only. Hands the selected ESC's signal line to the AM32 configurator through the Pico. This stops the boat and drops the serial link — then open am32.ca in Chromium on the Pi and connect to the Pico port. Restart the boat stack when done. See docs/esc_setup.md.
