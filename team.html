Co-founder

Kyle Tran

Hardware & systems

Kyle studies Mechanical Engineering with a minor in Robotics at Northeastern University, class of 2029, and grew up in Burlingame, California. On D.R.E.A.M.S he leads the hardware: the hull and CAD, the electronics and custom PCBs, and integrating it all into one working system.

Before the boat there was combat robotics. With Team DHM, from 2023 to 2025, he designed and 3D-printed around ten robots across five weight classes, placed 4th nationally in Plastic Antweight in 2024, and helped the club to 16 podium finishes over five events. Losing a match, working out why and coming back with a fix is still how he builds.

The first D.R.E.A.M.S was built in London and shown at the inaugural Creators' Showcase at Northeastern University London. Away from the boat he has built a 3D-printed robot that finds its own way through a maze, a printed RC plane and ceramics tools anyone can print for free. Next is a five-axis arm, three of its joints on cycloidal gearboxes, that will one day collect samples from the deck.

Co-founder

Matthew Hong

Mechanical, planning & communications

Matthew is a Mechanical Engineering student at Northeastern University with minors in Electrical Engineering and Business, on the PlusOne accelerated B.S./M.S. track. He spent his first year in London through the London Scholar Program, where he and Kyle built the first D.R.E.A.M.S.

On D.R.E.A.M.S he co-leads the mechanical and structural design in SolidWorks, including a Van Veen grab sediment sampler delivered with a full drawing package and BOM. He also handles planning and communications.

He has interned twice at Quanta Labs in Santa Clara, most recently on the shock and vibration team in summer 2026. There he ran more than 75 tests for aerospace, defense and industrial clients and built a Flask and SQLite app to track an inventory of more than 125 accelerometers. He is aiming for a career in aerospace, defense or Formula 1.

How we work

Four rules we build by.

They're also why this site looks the way it does.

  1. 01

    Real over rendered

    Everything on this site is something we actually designed, built, photographed or tested. Renders are only for what isn't built yet.

  2. 02

    Honest status

    Every part carries its real status, including the ones that failed. The dead ends taught us as much as the wins.

  3. 03

    Water decides

    A design isn't proven until it's been on the water. That's where V1 earned its place, and where V3 is headed.

  4. 04

    Fix what we learn

    Each version is a response to the last one. V3 exists because V2 showed us exactly what not to do.
