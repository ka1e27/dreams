In conversation

Port of Oakland

Sediment sampling

Our conversations with the Port of Oakland, and with the CRWA, showed us that sediment samples are still collected by sending divers down. That's shaping our long-term plan for a sample-collection arm.

03 · Ways to partner

Where you come in.

Three kinds of partners will shape the next stage. Each gets a direct line to the founders.

For research labs & faculty

Make the data count

Help us make our survey data scientifically useful.

  • Validate our probes against lab-grade instruments
  • Advise on sampling methods and data quality
  • Co-develop research uses for the survey data
For sponsors & industry

Put hardware on the water

Support goes straight into the boat.

  • Funding
  • Fabrication and machining
  • Engineering mentorship
For watershed groups & test sites

Survey your water

Have a pond, lake or reservoir you'd like to understand better?

  • Host supervised field trials
  • Receive the survey data we collect
  • Tell us what your watershed needs measured
