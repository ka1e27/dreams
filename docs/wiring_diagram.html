Build Notes

  • Run all logic-domain ground returns to a single star point at the buck array; do not bond logic GND to propulsion GND.
  • VESC master/slave CAN bus terminated 120 Ω at each VESC; stub length on Pico's UART branch under 30 cm.
  • BNO085 mounted to the hull keel, axes aligned to vehicle frame; magnetometer cal in clear of speakers and DC-DC.
  • SBUS receiver runs on its own 5 V buck — sharing with servos couples brownouts straight into the failsafe input.
  • Hull leak switches normally open; the pull-up is internal to the Pico (GP17/18). 1 kΩ in series limits fault current.
  • The 6S midpoint tap pulls current — never use this rail for high-cycle loads; the winch is intermittent and acceptable.
  • Pod-side power and UART share the same 4-conductor slip ring; the cable-side connector is JST-GH 4-pin with O-ring strain relief.
